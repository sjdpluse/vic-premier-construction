## Business description supplied by client

The business describes its positioning as premium residential and commercial construction and renovation across Melbourne, with emphasis on quality, detail, reliable workmanship, clear communication and durable results.

Services supplied by the client include:

- commercial and domestic works
- interior and exterior painting
- roof restoration
- gutter installation, repair and replacement
- tiling for kitchens, bathrooms and living areas
- wall rendering
- general carpentry for structural and finishing work

## Website objective

The website is primarily a lead-generation system, not an online brochure. Its main job is to convert relevant Melbourne visitors into qualified quote enquiries.

Primary conversion:

**Request a Free Quote**

Secondary conversions:

- Call the business
- Email the business
- View recent work
- Explore a relevant service

## Target audiences

1. Melbourne homeowners planning renovation, maintenance or improvement work.
2. Property owners/managers seeking specific trade and renovation services.
3. Commercial clients requiring construction, renovation or finishing work.

These audience descriptions are planning assumptions and must not be presented as facts about existing customers.

## Planned site map

## Homepage content hierarchy

1. Header / primary navigation
2. Hero: value proposition + Free Quote + Projects CTA
3. Quick proof / service-category strip
4. Intro / positioning
5. Services grid
6. Featured real projects
7. Why choose us — only factual, defensible qualities
8. Process: enquiry → assessment → quote → work → handover, phrased generically until client confirms operational process
9. Quote CTA
10. Contact / location block
11. Footer

## Quote form requirements

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Suburb / postcode
- Service required
- Project description
- Preferred timeframe
- Photo/plan upload

Do not collect information that is not needed to respond to the enquiry.
